## Spera

*Co-founder and design lead · Feb 2026 to now · remote*

Spera is a workspace to build, backtest and paper trade crypto strategies without hiring a developer. We are three founders. I own design and the frontend end to end: the look of the product from the first logo, the design system, the interface, and the workflows that turn a sketch, a spec or a bug report into shipped UI.

<GraphStat title="SPERA, SEP 2026">

- 334 commits to the production frontend
- **235 semantic design tokens**
- 180+ component specs
- 6 automated checks that hold the standard in CI

</GraphStat>

- **Logic Deck.** Strategies are authored as an ordered list of steps a trader can read top to bottom, not a freeform graph.
- **Honest backtests.** Quick, Deep and Compare; warnings at the moment of Run, never a hard gate; a verdict before the numbers.
- **An AI that proposes before it acts.** Approval is explicit and every agent-authored edit can be undone.
- **The system behind the screens.** Specs, tokens and audits move with the code, so the next change starts from what the last one learned.

Read the case: [Spera](/work/spera-platform/read).

## HP

*UX design engineer, Innovation for India · Aug 2023 to Feb 2026 · Bengaluru. R&D intern Feb to Jul 2023.*

HP is where I learned design at scale: AI that people have to trust, and a design system that hundreds of people build on. Most of this work is under NDA, so the case shows what can be shown.

<GraphStat title="HP">

- **500+ employees on Mudra, the design system I built**
- 15+ hackathon solutions taken to real teams
- 7+ cloud microservices
- millions of IoT printers watched on dashboards I designed

</GraphStat>

- **HP Print AI.** A key contributor to the industry's first intelligent print experience: consumer and business flows that turn opaque AI, hardware and cloud logic into steps people trust.
- **Mudra.** The design system and branding for HP's internal AI stack, in Figma and Next.js, with text-to-image prompts inside the stack.
- **Get My Prints, Print Learn Center, the onboarding app.** Doorstep printing on the mobile web, worksheets for parents over WhatsApp, and a first session with less drop-off.
- **As an R&D intern** I built a bot that generates Splunk dashboards and alerts for printer fleets.

Read the case: [HP, Innovation for India](/work/hp-print-ai/read).

Square Bridge puts students, recruiters and placement officers on one platform. I led its UX strategy and design with a team of three designers: more than 150 screens, tested with more than 200 candidates.

Read the case: [Square Bridge](/work/square-bridge/read).
