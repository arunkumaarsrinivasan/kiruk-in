excerpt: Treating the content layer as its own small engine so every surface plugs in the same way.

The blog stopped being *the system*. It's now one consumer of a tiny content
kernel — declare a schema, get typed entries, loud validation, one contract.

Notes are the proof: this page exists because of a schema and a route. No engine
changes. The next surfaces (progress, work, docs) join the same way.

> Seedling. Rough on purpose — the point is that it's *here*, not that it's done.
