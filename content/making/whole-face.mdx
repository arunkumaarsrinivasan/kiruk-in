## The rule

An era is a lens on the detail. It is never a replacement for it. Frost, blur, masks and fades are allowed while the eye is changing era, and nowhere else.

## Why

Two designs died for hiding the face. One redrew 1970 in characters. The other gave 2013 a frosted sheet over the eye at rest, the way that year's interfaces blurred what was behind them.

<Verdict on="2013 and 2026, second pass">

show all the details like 1970, not removing all

</Verdict>

The frost stayed. It moved into the cut: it lifts off as 2013 arrives and mists over as it leaves.

## The test

Stop on any era and look for six things: a brow hair, a pore, the lid fold, one lash, an iris fibre, the page in the cornea. If one is missing, the era is not finished.
