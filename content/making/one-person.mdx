## The rule

There is one person. They are 25, South Indian, with a dark brown iris. Every era shows that person.

## Why

The first plan gave each era its own face: someone around fifty for 1970, someone else for 2003. Ten well-made eyes in a row read as a gallery of strangers. One eye in ten kinds of light reads as a life spent in front of screens, which is the story the site is telling.

## What it costs

Every realism change has to be checked ten times, each in its own terms. A new skin detail that is right in 2009's warm photo can vanish in 1981's two colours or turn to noise in 1970's phosphor.

"Most realistic in 1995" therefore never means a change to the person. It means the same person under 1995's conditions: its light, the pupil that light causes, its lens, its medium.
