## What it was

Plain SVG and a spring. The pupil's position came from the cursor, and each era had its own small component that drew the same four shapes in its own manner: phosphor cells, a 1-bit bitmap, a hyperlink, candy gel, a flat icon.

The first-visit screen was a row of these. You scrolled along it and picked a year.

## What it left behind

Two things carried into every later eye. The first is the idea itself: the era is a way of drawing, and the subject stays the same. The second is the input. Sweep to travel through time, tap the eye to enter. The loader still works that way.

## Why it was not enough

A mark can look at you. It cannot be looked into. The picker needed something a visitor would stay with for a few seconds, and a logo is read in one.
