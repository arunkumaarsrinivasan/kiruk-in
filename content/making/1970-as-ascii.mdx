## What was asked

"Fine-tune the era looks, starting with 1970, 1981 and 1991."

## What was built

Redesigns. 1970 was redrawn as rows of dot-matrix characters, the way a terminal would print a picture. 1981 was put inside an early window.

<Verdict on="the three redesigns">

it just misses so much detail… changed the entire vibe… all the things have already been used

</Verdict>

All three were reverted.

## What it taught

Two rules came out of this round.

The first is about the work: [the whole face stays visible](/making/whole-face). A terminal's characters are too coarse to hold a lash or a pore, so a face drawn in them stops being a face.

The second is about the process. "Fine-tune" is not permission to redesign. A look that has been signed off keeps its medium and all of its detail unless the change is named.

[The 1970 that shipped](/making/1970-phosphor) keeps the phosphor and loses nothing.
