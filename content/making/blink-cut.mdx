## The problem

Ten pictures of one eye have to change into each other. A crossfade shows two faces at once. A slide turns the eye into a card. Both admit that these are images being swapped.

## The move

The eye blinks, and the cut happens while it is shut.

<GraphTimeline title="ONE CUT">

- Look: for 70 ms the eye glances the way you are travelling
- Close: the lids come down while the old era leaves in its own way
- **Shut: the swap. Light, lens, page, sign and gaze all change at once, unseen**
- Open: the new era arrives in its own way as the lids lift
- Ready: its sign appears last

</GraphTimeline>

A fast sweep across several eras keeps the eye shut until the hand slows. Nothing flashes past.

## Where the idea came from

Three rival concepts for the transition were written and three judges chose between them. All three picked the blink independently. It was the only one where the transition was something the subject does, not something done to it.
