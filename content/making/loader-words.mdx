## The job

A visitor lands on an eye. They need to know that it can be scrolled through time and clicked to enter. The obvious answer is to tell them.

## The rounds

- **An eye study.** Readouts around the eye, like a specimen sheet.
- **A U-shaped orbit.** The eras' names running in an arc under the eye.
- **Edge bands and slices.** The neighbouring eras peeking in at the left and right.
- **Capsules at the corners of the eye.** Then lines and ticks leaving them.
- **Instruction without text.** The eye acting it out, the cornea as a menu, a ring on the cursor.

## Why they all failed

Each one added a layer between the visitor and the eye. The layers differed. The mistake was the same: something laid on top, explaining.

## What replaced them

[No words on the eye](/making/no-words). The message moved into the thing itself: each era's own picture technology says which era you are in, and one sign in the pupil says it opens.
