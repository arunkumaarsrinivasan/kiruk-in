## What it taught

That realism in an eye is a list. Skin relief, the wet rim, vessels in the white, the depth of the iris, the way lashes clump. The realism pass went through that list one part at a time and asked of each: take it, improve it, or leave it.

## How it was used

The repository carries no licence, so no code or texture came across. The parts were rebuilt here from anatomy, from the papers listed on [the eye's page](/making/loader-eye), and from [a photograph of my own eye](/making/real-eye-photo).

It is on this map because a lineage that hides its references is not one.
