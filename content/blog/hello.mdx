This blog is a **sketchbook**. It's where the pen-and-paper thinking, the half-formed
ideas, and the digital refinements live side by side. Less polished essay, more open
notebook.

## Pen, then pixels

The interesting part is the *translation*. What survives the jump from paper to screen,
and what gets fixed along the way?

<Compare
  pen="/blog/hello/flow-pen.png"
  digital="/blog/hello/flow-digital.png"
  caption="Notebook flow (left) → the cleaned-up digital version (right)."
/>

Some things I hold onto in that jump:

- The **rough hierarchy** — what's big stays big.
- The dumb-but-honest labels I wrote at 1am.
- Any arrow I drew twice, because that's where the real logic was.

> The manual tells you *how*. The sketchbook tells you *why* — and shows the wrong turns
> that got cut before anyone saw them.
