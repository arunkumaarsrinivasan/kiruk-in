# Voice reference

Canonical: `content/blog/life/variation-keeps-me-alive.mdx`.

## Sounds like him

```text
I tried that. I got bored inside my own life.

I'm not selling a method. I just want to tell you how I'm trying to live.

I'm just saying, if every shower is the same shower, you stop feeling the shower…

Gym people will tell you consistency is the whole game… I'm not arguing with them.

It won't be perfect, and it won't be good enough as you think.
```

## Sounds like a rewrite he rejected

```text
I'm a mountain person. That part is settled.

I don't owe a genre loyalty.

I'm open to explore. I don't confine myself to a definition.

Trying more won't dilute what's yours.
```

These are clean, declarative, self-branding. Prefer the softer spoken equivalents from the post (mountain as home + explore anyway; genre as "I don't stick to one like I owe it something"; water / confine near the end, not as a mic drop every section).

## Rhythm

- Alternate short hits with longer spoken sentences.
- Lists of concrete things are good: brushes, pastes, shoes, places.
- Contrasts sit after the story beat lands (weird menu → then gym people), not before.
- Questions people ask go in `>` blockquotes; answer in his voice under them.

## Life essay vs craft notebook

| Life (`domain: [life]`) | Craft / method / walk |
| --- | --- |
| Scene H2s: morning, travel, work | Craft → Method → Walk spines |
| Feelings + small rituals | Sketch / Build / Seam, Intent / Parts |
| Marks for emphasis | InkPage / Figure / Compare |

Same voice either way. Different skeleton.

## Reader promise

He is not teaching a system. He is showing how he tries to live / make, and inviting a try. Keep "maybe I'm right, maybe I'm wrong" energy when the section gets preachy.
